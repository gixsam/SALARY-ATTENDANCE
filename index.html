How to Extract Attendance Data from ZKTeco Device

Follow either of the two standard official methods below:

Method 1: Direct USB Flash Drive Extraction
  1. Insert a FAT32-formatted USB PenDrive into your ZKTeco machine.
  2. Press the Menu (M/OK) button on the keypad.
  3. Navigate to USB Manager / PenDrive and press OK.
  4. Select Download Attendance Data and press OK.
  5. The machine saves an attendance log file (e.g. 1_attlog.dat) onto your USB.
  6. Open the file in Notepad, copy all text, and paste using "Paste Log"!
Method 2: Export via ZKTime / BioTime Software
  1. Open ZKTime or BioTime software on your office PC.
  2. Go to Attendance Calculation, select the required month, and click Calculate.
  3. Click the Export button and save as Excel (.xlsx) or Text.
  4. Copy columns: Employee ID, Date, Check-In, Check-Out and paste here.

Pro Tip: General office duty is 09:00 AM – 07:00 PM (10 hours max). Multi-shift workers (e.g. Control Cell) get accurate shift calculations automatically as approved by Management.

Direct Formulas for Microsoft Excel / WPS Office

1. Hourly Rate Formula (Based on 248 Standard Working Hours)

=ROUND(Basic_Salary / 248, 2)

2. Standard Capping (09:00 AM - 07:00 PM)

=IF(OR(B5="", C5=""), 0, MAX(0, MIN(C5, TIME(19,0,0)) - MAX(B5, TIME(9,0,0))))
